QC fixtures
===========
sim/*.ab1          Synthetic Sanger traces generated by make_qc_fixtures.py (own code; deterministic seeds). Controls: nr2f2_control, apoe_control.
sim/truth.json     Allele mixtures used to build each edited trace (label, fraction, indel size, sequence), guides, donor, cut indices.
sim/oracle_ice.json  Output of Synthego ICE 1.2.0 on the same traces (summary, contributions, outcomes). Reference only.
oracle_ice_examples.json  ICE output on the traces shipped with ICE (not redistributed); matches ICE's own test expectations
                   (good example ICE 77; +14 knock-in HDR 33; 2 bp substitution HDR 44; multiplex ICE_d 66/67; 3 guides ICE 63).
Edited traces follow the linear mixture model: at each base position the channel heights are the total signal times the sum of the
fractions of the alleles carrying that base. Real traces add noise, dye-mobility shifts and read-through artefacts not modelled here.
