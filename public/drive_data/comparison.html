MYD88 pools: reverse read requires review

1172 agrees within 1.2 percentage points for editing. For 1173, Studio estimates 96.9% edited versus ICE 88% (+8.9 points); KO scores differ by 13.5 points. Studio’s reverse-read fit is weaker (R² 0.856 versus 0.978 for 1172). ICE also flags its short inference window. Neither estimate is established as the truth. Native high-confidence labels do not resolve this disagreement. Pools remain on hold and do not establish clone genotypes.

Next product improvements supported by these data

  1. Make exact control identity and experiment provenance visible before analysis; block substitutions that would silently change the comparison.
  2. Show sequence-level KO predictions separately from functional knockout confirmation, including in-frame large deletions.
  3. Keep fit quality, intervals and cross-method disagreements beside the decision label.
  4. Require the actual guide–donor alternative before reporting a definitive intended-edit percentage.
